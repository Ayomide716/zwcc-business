2026 ZWCC
Business Grant

Funding to help you start and grow a business that lasts. Apply from your phone in one sitting.

Download the app Android · 120 MB · use Wi-Fi if you can

How to install

  1. 1

    Tap Download the app and wait for it to finish.

  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file. Your phone may say installing from this source is not allowed. Tap Settings, turn on “Allow from this source”, then press back.

  3. 3

    Tap Install, then Open.

  4. 4

    Create your account and allow notifications. We will tell you when your application moves forward, so you do not have to keep checking.

Why your phone shows a warning The app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks permission before installing it. This is normal, and step 2 covers it.

What to have ready

  • A recent passport photograph
  • A valid means of identification — NIN slip, driver’s licence, voter’s card or passport
  • Your business registration document
  • What your business does, and what you would use the grant for

You can photograph documents with your phone. The application saves as you go, so you can stop and come back to it later.

Who can apply

Anyone aged 18 or over with a business, or a clear business idea. You do not have to be a member of the church. There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant — you ask for what your proposal needs.

The app is Android only for now. If you have an iPhone, ask someone with an Android phone to help, or speak to the grant team.
