2026 ZWCC Business Grant

Zion World Christian Center · Lagos

Apply from your phone

Zion World Christian Center provides grants to help people start and grow businesses that last. Use this app to apply, follow your application, and report on your progress. You do not have to be a member of the church.

Download the app Android · about 120 MB · use Wi‑Fi if you can
Your phone will ask permission first This app is not on the Play Store yet, so Android will warn you before installing it. That warning is normal for an app installed directly. The steps below cover it.

How to install

  1. Tap Download the app above and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone may say installing from this source is not allowed — tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Create an account with your email address, and allow notifications so you hear about your application without having to check.

What you will need

  • A recent passport photograph
  • A valid means of identification — NIN slip, driver’s licence, voter’s card or passport
  • Your business registration document
  • Details of your business and what you would use the grant for

You can photograph documents with your phone. The application saves as you go, so you can stop and come back to it.

iPhone

The app is Android only for now. If you have an iPhone, ask someone with an Android phone to help, or contact the grant team.
